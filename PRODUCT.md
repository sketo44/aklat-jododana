# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS (single page, no build step). Chosen because the site is a small venue page that must load fast on mobile data and deploy to any static host.

## Users

People in Buraydah (Qassim) deciding where to eat traditional Saudi food, mostly on their phone. Their job: see what the restaurant serves and what it costs, check it is open, and get directions so they can visit in person.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for "أكلات جدودنا" (Jododana Restaurant). Success means a visitor leaves knowing the menu, the opening hours, and the way there, and taps Directions or Call.

## Positioning

Traditional home-style Saudi dishes (rice-based cooked dishes, tomato and spice stews) served under the name "our grandparents' food". The heritage name is the restaurant's own claim.

## Operating Context

- Dine-in visit is the primary conversion; table and event bookings second. Delivery ordering exists in delivery apps but is secondary.
- Two branches in Buraydah:

  - Sultanah: طريق الملك سلمان، حي سلطانة، phone 016 380 4000, open from after Fajr to 1:00 AM (third-party listing, unconfirmed).
- Events/occasions phone: 050 384 4000 (from the restaurant's Snapchat profile).
- Social: Snapchat @gododana; Facebook page facebook.com/61562399023446. Old domain gdodna.com no longer resolves.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Arabic RTL primary, English toggle (user requirement).
- Three pages: intro, menu, reservations (user requirement).
- Reservations are a request handed off to WhatsApp/phone; no booking backend exists.
- Undecided: confirmed opening hours, which number takes WhatsApp bookings, vector logo.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: أكلات جدودنا / Jododana Restaurant.
- User-pinned: heritage style following the existing logo system and colours, luxurious feel. Logo = white geometric Kufi-style lettering on a deep maroon field; facade = Najdi mud plaster with a maroon sign band and a white-triangle frieze.

## Evidence on Hand

- Full menu with prices: delivery-app screenshots in the owner's folder (OneDrive Desktop/اجدادنا), 34 items, 26 with dish photos cropped to assets/dishes/.
- Facade night photo from Google Maps (assets/facade.jpg).
- Prices shown are regular prices; app discounts are temporary promotions and are not shown.
- Google rating 3.8 (public). Do not quote reviews or invent testimonials, dish names, or prices.

## Product Principles

1. Visiting is the goal: hours, location, and call/directions are always one tap away.
2. Real food, real prices: never show invented dishes or numbers.
3. Mobile first: most visitors decide on a phone, often on the road.
4. Heritage claim stays honest: draw on Najdi/Qassim food culture without kitsch or borrowed symbols.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic RTL first, readable on small screens in daylight, WCAG AA contrast.
