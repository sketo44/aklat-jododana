## Scope

Three-page bilingual (Arabic RTL default, English toggle) static site: index.html (intro, Persuade), menu.html (Read), reservations.html (Operate, booking request).

## Audience and job

Buraydah diners on phones, often evening. Understand the place, scan real dishes and prices, find the branch, book a table or an event.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the restaurant's own facade: mud-plaster walls, a maroon sign band carrying illuminated white Kufi lettering, and the white-triangle frieze. Refuses the category default of a dark food-photo hero with gold script and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Mud plaster ground (ochre-tan, textured), deep maroon sign bands, sign-white lettering, frieze of white triangles on maroon as the only ornament, night-sky umber for the hero sky. Reem Kufi for the sign voice, Readex Pro for text. Square corners like plaster blocks.

STORY: A visitor sees the real building and sign, knows it serves Qassim home dishes, sees real plates with real prices, then books or gets directions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The facade photo fills the frame, nav set into a maroon band at top. A maroon sign plaque overlaps the photo's lower third holding the name at display scale, one line of hook, and two actions: book a table (primary, sign-white on maroon edge) and directions. The frieze closes the viewport edge.

FORM: Brief-pinned world (owner's facade, logo and colours); no seed roll. Signature interaction: the sign lights up on load (letters go from unlit plaster to glowing white, like channel letters at dusk); menu category rail tracks the section in view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
